Common questions

What counts as a computer?
Each PC where the add-in is activated uses one seat. The same person on two PCs uses two seats.
A computer died or was replaced. Do I lose a seat?
No. On the new PC, open 100x Pro Tools → Licence, enter your key, and pick the old computer from the list to free its seat. The old PC stops working; the new one starts. Up to three moves per month.
Does it need the internet?
Only to activate and for a quick check once a day. It keeps working offline for up to 14 days.
What happens when the subscription ends?
The tools stop, but every sheet, template and document you created stays in your files and keeps working.
Mac or Excel on the web?
The .xlam is Windows only. The free web edition works everywhere.
